          DIGITAL SAFETY

          Stay informed. Stay safer.

          Learn to recognize common manipulation techniques.

          Digital arrest scams

          Fraudsters impersonate police or government officials, threaten arrest and demand money. Do not transfer money because of a threatening call.

          Banking and UPI scams

          Never share your UPI PIN, OTP or banking password. Be cautious of collect requests, fake refunds and remote-access application instructions.

          Fake jobs and internships

          Verify recruiters independently. A demand for a registration fee to secure an internship deserves careful scrutiny.

          Voice impersonation

          A familiar-sounding voice is not proof of identity. Independently contact the person through a previously saved number before sending money.

          Suspicious links

          Inspect the actual domain carefully. Do not enter credentials after following unexpected links.

          Urgency and isolation

          Scammers often pressure victims to act quickly and discourage them from consulting family or trusted people.
