SCAM LENS / PRIVACY

Privacy & Data Use

What is processed when you use Scam Lens, what stays in your browser, and what you should avoid submitting.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. About this notice

Scam Lens is an AI-assisted scam-awareness prototype. It reviews submitted content for possible social-engineering warning signs and provides informational suggestions. It does not authenticate a person, sender, organisation, phone number or website, and it cannot guarantee that content is safe or fraudulent.

This notice describes the current application implementation. The services used by a future deployment may change, so this page should be reviewed whenever its processing flow changes.

2. What is sent for processing

  • Text analysis: The message text and selected language preference are sent from the Scam Lens server to the configured Groq chat model for analysis.
  • Screenshot analysis: The browser prepares a compressed image and sends it to the Scam Lens server. The server forwards the image to the configured Groq vision model to extract visible text. If you choose to analyze the extracted text, that text is then sent for text analysis.
  • Voice analysis: The recording or audio file is sent to the Scam Lens server and forwarded to the configured Groq transcription service. The transcript is shown for review. If you choose to analyze it, the transcript is then sent to the chat model. This feature transcribes speech; it does not verify the caller's identity or determine whether a voice is cloned.
  • Link inspection: The submitted URL is sent to the Scam Lens server for structural parsing. This implementation does not open the destination, fetch the website, or query a live malicious-domain reputation service.

Avoid submitting information that is not necessary for an assessment. In particular, do not submit passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, full payment-card numbers or full bank credentials.

3. Third-party AI processing

Text, screenshot and audio processing uses Groq-hosted AI services configured by the application owner. The relevant submitted content is transmitted to those services to complete the requested operation. Groq handles that data under its own applicable terms and privacy practices. Scam Lens does not control independent third-party retention or processing, so do not submit sensitive material.

4. Server-side storage

In this version, Scam Lens does not provide user accounts or a server-side database for saved reports. The application code processes requests and returns results; it does not intentionally save submitted messages, screenshots, recordings or reports in an application database.

The hosting platform and third-party services may process technical request data under their own policies. Error diagnostics may be written to the server console; the application should not be treated as a confidential document-storage service.

5. History in your browser

Reports are saved only when you explicitly select the application's Save locally action. The app stores up to the 15 most recent saved reports in browser local storage. A saved report includes the submitted text, its analysis and the save timestamp.

The History screen lets you delete individual reports or use Delete all to clear saved reports from that browser. Saved reports are not synced through a Scam Lens account because the current version has no account system.

Anyone with access to the same browser profile or device may be able to view locally saved reports. Clearing browser site data also removes local application data. Deleting a local report does not delete data that a third-party provider may independently retain under its terms.

Rescue Mode: The incident-summary form is generated in your browser. The app does not send the information entered in these fields to the Scam Lens server or its AI providers, and does not add it to saved report history or browser local storage. If you select Copy summary, your browser's clipboard receives the summary. Avoid entering passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or full financial identifiers.

6. Audio recording and consent

The voice feature may request microphone permission from your browser. Only record or upload conversations that you are legally allowed to record and share. You are responsible for obtaining any consent required by the laws that apply to you.

7. Limitations and safe use

  • A result with no obvious warning signs is not proof of authenticity.
  • OCR and speech transcription can make mistakes; review extracted text.
  • A URL without structural warnings may still lead to a malicious website.
  • Do not use an AI result as the only basis for a financial or security decision.
  • Verify important requests through contact details you find independently.

8. Reporting suspected cyber fraud in India

If money has been transferred or debited in a suspected cyber fraud, contact your bank or payment provider immediately and call the national cyber financial fraud helpline at 1930. You can also report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in.
